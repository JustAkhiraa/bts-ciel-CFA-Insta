Cybersecurity when working from home · les exercices

Cinq temps :
identifier les documents, maîtriser leurs mots, comprendre la vidéo, répondre à la gérante, puis présenter le dossier en cinq minutes. Le cours · La fiche

2What does the title of document A tell us? Moyen

Le titre du document A est Coronavirus stimulus scams are here. How to identify these new online and text attacks. Que dit-il du contenu de l'article ? Répondre en anglais.

It tells us that scammers are using the emergency money paid by governments during the pandemic, the stimulus, to trick people. The attacks come through the Internet and text messages, and the article explains how to recognise them.

4Efficient or effective? Moyen

Compléter chaque phrase par efficient ou effective.

  1. Two-factor authentication is very ___ against stolen passwords.
  2. This new laptop is more ___: its battery lasts twice as long.
  3. Training is the most ___ way to stop phishing.
  4. An ___ backup system saves time and storage space.
✅ Correction
  1. effective :
    elle atteint son but, arrêter les mots de passe volés.
  2. efficient :
    le même travail avec moins d'énergie.
  3. effective : c'est ce qui marche le mieux.
  4. efficient :
    on économise du temps et de la place.

La règle :
effective dit que ça marche, efficient dit que ça marche sans gaspiller.

5Why is cybersecurity a major concern? Moyen

Why is cybersecurity a major concern in the report?

✅ Correction

Because thousands of people suddenly work from home, many of them for the first time, and scammers take advantage of the situation: phishing scams are on the rise.

6Why are people more vulnerable? Moyen

According to the tech expert, why are people more vulnerable to phishing at that time?

✅ Correction

Because they are scared. When people are frightened, they act quickly, without thinking things through, so they click on fake links more easily.

7Five good practices Facile

List five good practices given by the tech expert.

✅ Correction
  1. Follow your company's IT recommendations.
  2. Avoid public Wi-Fi.
  3. Make sure your home Wi-Fi is password-protected.
  4. Update your operating system.
  5. Check for malware and have it removed.

Et le conseil qui les résume :
if in doubt, take a deep breath and call a professional.

8How does the lawyer's firm stay safe? Facile

What does the lawyer's firm do to stay safe?

✅ Correction

It works with a technical consultant, and it has bank-level security on its server.

4Le dialogue avec la gérante

Le dialogue travaillé en classe :
la gérante parle, tu es le technicien (IT technician). Réponds à voix haute, puis compare avec la réplique modèle.

Les trois questions du sujet y sont :
protéger les données aux exercices 9 et 11, l'antivirus au 10, la charte au 14.

9Protect the company Moyen

Manager :
As you know, all our 40 employees are currently working from home because of the COVID-19 lockdown. Therefore, I'm very worried about cyberattacks and online scams. What can we do to protect our company?

✅ Correction

IT technician :
You're right to be worried: scammers are using the crisis to trick people. I suggest three kinds of protection. First, software: an up-to-date antivirus, updates and a VPN on every laptop. Second, hardware: only company laptops, no personal computers. Third, people: training and clear rules for everyone.

10Antivirus programs Moyen

Manager :
How effective are antivirus programs?

✅ Correction

IT technician :
They are useful, but they are not enough. An antivirus detects known malware, so it must be updated all the time. But it can't stop an employee who types their password on a fake website, because there is no virus. That's why we also need two-factor authentication and training.

11Sensitive accounting data Difficile

Manager :
What about protecting our sensitive accounting data?

✅ Correction

IT technician :
Our accounting data should stay on the company server, not on personal computers. Employees will connect to it through a VPN, which encrypts the connection. Each person will only access the files they need. And we'll back up the data every day, with one copy offline, so that ransomware can't destroy everything.

12Hardware solutions Difficile

Manager :
Are there any hardware solutions?

✅ Correction

IT technician : Yes, several. We can give each employee a company laptop with an encrypted disk, so a stolen laptop reveals nothing. We can also use security keys: small USB keys that prove who you are when you log in. At the office, a firewall protects the server, and a backup drive keeps a copy of our data.

13Fake emails Moyen

Manager :
I'm also worried about employees receiving fake emails or suspicious messages. How can we prevent that?

✅ Correction

IT technician :
We can't stop all of them, but we can reduce them. An email filter blocks most phishing messages. Then I'll train the staff to spot the warning signs: an urgent tone, a strange sender address, an unexpected link or attachment, a request for a password or money. Above all, they must report a suspicious message to me instead of clicking on it.

14The data charter Difficile

Manager :
What exactly is the data charter?

✅ Correction

IT technician :
It's a document that sets the rules for using the company's computers and data: strong passwords, a locked screen, no public Wi-Fi without the VPN, no software installed without permission, and every incident reported. Every employee reads it and signs it. So everybody knows the rules, and we can show that we protect our clients' personal data, as the GDPR requires.

15The security plan Facile

Manager : Thank you for your advice. Please prepare a security plan for the company as soon as possible.

✅ Correction

IT technician : Of course. I'll send you a plan by Friday, with the priorities, the costs and a timetable. The most urgent steps are the VPN and the updates; the training can start next week.

⚠️ Juste en anglais, et juste techniquement

Un VPN chiffre le trajet entre l'ordinateur et le cabinet. Il n'arrête pas les virus et ne remplace pas l'antivirus : ne lui fais pas dire plus.

De même, une charte n'arrête aucune attaque. Elle sert parce qu'elle fixe des habitudes que tout le monde a signées.

💡 Deux ou trois phrases par réplique

À l'oral, une réponse courte et claire vaut mieux qu'un paragraphe appris. L'examinateur relance s'il veut en savoir plus.

Une structure qui marche à chaque fois :
la réponse, une raison, un exemple.

5La présentation, rédigée

Un modèle pour les cinq minutes d'expression en continu :
l'introduction et la question du dossier, le document A, le document B en trois temps, puis le lien entre les deux et un avis.

présentation orale · 410 mots
Good morning. Today I'm going to
present a file about cybersecurity
when people work from home. It is
made of two documents. Document A
is an article about scams linked
to the coronavirus. Document B is
a short news report by 41 Action
News, a local American TV channel.
Both documents deal with the
Covid-19 lockdown, when millions
of people started working from
home. They raise one question: how
can a company protect its data
when its staff are not in the
office?
[83 mots]

Let me start with document A. Its
title is "Coronavirus stimulus
scams are here. How to identify
these new online and text
attacks". A stimulus is money that
a government gives to people
during a crisis. The title
suggests that criminals use this
money as bait: they send fake
messages, online or by text, to
steal money or personal
information. The article also
promises to explain how to
recognise these attacks.
[71 mots]

Moving on to document B. The
report starts with a lawyer who is
working from home for the first
time in his career, with all the
employees of his firm. He shows
his new office: a laptop, a work
table and pictures of his family.
According to him, technology makes
our lives easier, and he has time
to learn how to use it.
[63 mots]

Then the report explains that
cybersecurity has become a major
concern. A tech expert says that
phishing scams are on the rise,
because frightened people tend to
act without thinking. The expert
gives a list of good practices:
follow your company's IT rules,
avoid public Wi-Fi, protect your
home Wi-Fi with a password, update
your operating system and remove
malware. Above all, if you have a
doubt, take a deep breath and call
a professional.
[75 mots]

That is exactly what the lawyer
does: his firm has a technical
consultant and bank-level security
on its server. The report ends on
a positive note: working from home
is a new reality, but the
technology has existed for years,
and people can adapt.
[44 mots]

To conclude, the two documents
complete each other. Document A
shows the threat: scammers take
advantage of the crisis. Document
B shows the answer: simple habits,
and help from IT professionals. In
my opinion, the weakest point of a
company is not its computers but
its people, because one click on a
fake link is enough. That is why,
as an IT technician, I would
combine technical tools with
training. Thank you for listening.
[74 mots]

410 mots

À une voix posée, autour de 110 mots par minute, ces 410 mots durent un peu moins de 4 minutes. La marge sous les 5 minutes est voulue :
à l'oral, on hésite, on reprend, et le temps file.

💡 Ce qui est à toi : l'avis

Les documents se présentent comme le modèle. L'avis de la fin doit être le tien :
garde la structure, pas l'opinion.

Une opinion se justifie :
In my opinion, ... because ...
