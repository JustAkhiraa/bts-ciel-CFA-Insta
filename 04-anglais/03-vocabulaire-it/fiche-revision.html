⚡ Le courriel professionnel, phrase par phrase

ObjetSubject: Request for information regarding…
AppelDear Ms Martin, — sans point sur Ms
MotifI am writing to inquire about…
MotiverI am particularly interested in your organization due to its strong reputation in…
DemanderI would be grateful if you could provide me with more information about…
Pièce jointePlease find my CV and cover letter attached to this email.
DisponibilitéI am available for an interview at your convenience.
OuvrirIf you require any further details, please do not hesitate to contact me.
Se vendreI am highly motivated and able to work both independently and as part of a team.
I am also used to meeting deadlines and handling multiple tasks simultaneously.
RemercierThank you in advance for your time and consideration.
AttendreI look forward to hearing from you soon.
PolitesseYours sincerely, — le nom est connu

Deux -ing obligatoires : look forward to hearing et used to meeting — dans les deux cas, to est une préposition.

Et le sujet d'examen propose ce courriel au choix, en 200 mots ± 10 % : un point du sujet = un paragraphe, dans l'ordre donné.

⚡ Deux pièges de forme

Nom qualifiant — nom + nom, le premier au singulier : child labour, conflict resolution, leadership skills, a two-hour meeting.

Génitif — appartenance, avec 's : children's books, Google's workforce, the company's debts.

Le test : peut-on dire « of » ?
oui → génitif · non → nom qualifiant

to rise monte tout seul (intransitif) · to raise monte quelque chose (transitif) : rising labour costs.
