The future of cybersecurity · les exercices

Quatre temps : identifier le document, maîtriser ses mots, comprendre ses idées, puis le compte rendu en anglais, rédigé et compté. Le cours · La fiche

1Identifier le document

1Nature, source, date, thème Facile

Pour l'introduction du compte rendu, donner en anglais la nature du document, sa source, sa date et son thème.

✅ Correction

Nature : a short video, made of interviews with cybersecurity experts.

Source : CNBC, an American business news channel, on YouTube.

Date : not given, à chercher sous la vidéo.

Thème : the future of cybersecurity with connected objects.

2Le vocabulaire

2Traduire les mots du thème Facile

Donner le sens de : wearable device, critical infrastructure, legacy system, to take over remotely, a standard, to depend on.

✅ Correction

wearable device : un objet connecté porté sur soi. critical infrastructure : les infrastructures vitales. legacy system : un système ancien encore en service.

to take over remotely : prendre le contrôle à distance. a standard : une norme. to depend on : dépendre de.

3Comprendre la vidéo

Réponds en anglais, avec tes mots : c'est déjà l'entraînement à la reformulation.

3Why is cybersecurity becoming a physical problem? Moyen

Why do the experts say that cybersecurity is becoming a physical problem?

✅ Correction

Because of the Internet of Things: connected objects, wearable devices and critical infrastructure are part of the real world, so an attack on them has real, physical consequences.

4The two issues of the IoT Moyen

What two issues does the first expert see with the Internet of Things?

✅ Correction

First, it brings cybersecurity into the physical world. Second, the diversity of platforms, operating systems and old environments makes security more complex, and it changes faster.

5The car and the fridge Moyen

Why is a connected car worrying, and why is a connected fridge less worrying?

✅ Correction

A car is almost a computer on wheels: a hacker could control its acceleration, its brakes or its airbags from a distance. A fridge that adds milk to a shopping list does not put anyone in danger.

Le critère, donné par la vidéo : the data, and what you can do with it.

6Why not wait for standards? Difficile

Why do the experts think the industry cannot wait for standards?

✅ Correction

Because creating standards and getting them adopted takes a long time. Meanwhile, connected objects are already everywhere, so problems must be fixed now, before we depend on them completely.

4Le compte rendu, rédigé

Un modèle, aux normes de l'épreuve : une introduction de 35 mots au plus, un développement de 130 à 150 mots, 200 mots à 10 % près en tout.
